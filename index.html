My Love Letter To You 💌

There are some things that are easier to write than to say.

Happy Birthday, my Muqquuu Jann. ❤️

I honestly don't know where to start because when it comes to you, somehow words never feel enough. I can write a hundred things about you and still feel like I haven't said even half of what I actually feel.

You are not just someone I love. You have become someone I look forward to, someone I think about during random moments of the day, someone whose smile can change my entire mood without even trying.

And Muqaddas, I want you to know something today... you are incredibly special to me.

I love the way you talk, the way you smile, the little expressions you make, the way you sometimes get annoyed, the way you care, and even those little things you probably don't think anyone notices. I notice them.

And then there are your eyes...

"Teri aankhon mein kuch aisi baat hai,
ke lafz bhi khamosh ho jaate hain...

Tujhe dekhoon toh waqt ruk sa jaata hai,
aur dil bas itna kehta hai...

Kaash ye pal kabhi khatam na ho. ❤️ "

And your hair... I don't even know how to explain it properly. Sometimes I just look at you and think how unfairly beautiful Allah has made you.

Your eyes, your smile, your hair, your voice, your little habits, your personality... somehow all of these things come together and make you.

But honestly, it's not just your beauty that makes me love you. It's the person you are underneath all of it.

I want to be there for you when you're happy, when you're stressed, when you're angry, when you're tired, and especially when you don't feel like talking.

I don't promise that life will always be perfect. But I promise that I want to keep choosing you through the imperfect days too.

And maybe one day, when we look back at this website, we'll laugh and say, "Look how much we loved each other even back then."

Until that day... keep smiling, keep being the beautiful Muqaddas I know, and please never forget that somewhere in this world, there is someone who is extremely grateful that you exist.

Yours,
Your future husband... hopefully. ❤️
